# Fortune Investment Services — Insurance Proposal Workspace

An interactive, multi-category insurance comparison, proposal builder, and advisor workspace developed for **Fortune Investment Services Pvt Ltd**.

---

## 🌟 Overview

The **Insurance Proposal Workspace** enables wealth managers and insurance advisors to compare flagship insurance plans across Health, Term, Motor, and Travel categories, customize parameters based on client needs, generate client-ready proposals, and track advisor pipelines.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏥 **Health Insurance**: Compare plans with unlimited restoration, no room rent capping, PED waiting periods, and network hospital counts.
- 🛡️ **Term Insurance**: Pure term and return-of-premium (ROP) options with smoker and age loading calculators.
- 🚗 **Motor Insurance**: Comprehensive and third-party coverage options with add-on benefits.
- ✈️ **Travel Insurance**: International single-trip and multi-trip medical and trip protection plans.
- 📊 **Structured Data & Content**: Explicit separation between quantitative metrics (financial grids, parameters, comparison tables) and qualitative content (features, target audience suitability, terms, disclaimers).
- 🖨️ **Export & Sharing**: Save client proposals as high-resolution images or export directly to PDF for presentation.
- 🔐 **Multi-User Access**: Built-in authentication supporting Admin and Team Advisor roles with proposal persistence.
